Respawn Report

Respawn Report publishes short, daily news videos about video games and gaming technology on YouTube: youtube.com/@RespawnReportNews.

About this application

youtube-robotu is a private tool used only by the owner of the Respawn Report channel. It prepares original, AI-assisted news Shorts (script, voice-over, captions and royalty-free stock footage) and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

The tool does not upload content to anyone else's channel, does not collect data from other users and does not re-upload videos made by other creators.
